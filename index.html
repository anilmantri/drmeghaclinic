Frequently Asked

Common Questions

How do I book an appointment?+
Book via the contact form on this page, WhatsApp us at +91 98765 43210, or call directly. Walk-ins are welcome subject to availability.
Is the initial consultation free?+
Yes — we offer a complimentary 15-minute initial consultation for first-time patients to assess your concern and recommend the right treatment plan.
How many PRP sessions are required?+
Most patients see results after 3–6 monthly sessions. Maintenance every 3–6 months is recommended to sustain long-term results.
Is hair transplant painful?+
FUE is performed under local anaesthesia. Most patients feel minimal discomfort during the procedure. Post-procedure soreness resolves within a few days.
What is the downtime after laser treatment?+
Surface peels cause mild redness for 1–2 days; ablative resurfacing may require 5–7 days. Dr. Megha will advise you fully before any treatment.
Do you treat teenage acne?+
Absolutely. We have specific protocols for teenage skin that are safe, effective, and appropriate — addressing both active acne and preventing future scarring.
